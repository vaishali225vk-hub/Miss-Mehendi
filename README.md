# Vaishu Mehndi Artistry - Luxury Bridal Henna Website

A mobile- and laptop-responsive luxury portfolio and booking website designed with senior UI/UX and graphic design principles, traditional Indian motifs, and automated WhatsApp booking integration.

---

## 🌺 Key Features

1. **Authentic Indian Mehndi Aesthetic**:
   - **Color Palette**: Deep Henna Burgundy (`#4A1521`), Royal Crimson (`#5C1318`), Antique Brushed Gold (`#C5A059`), and Warm Raw Silk Ecru (`#FAF7F2`) with Herbal Eucalyptus Sage accents (`#3B4238`).
   - **Traditional Motifs**: Architectural Jharokha (royal arches), Jaali (lattice patterns), Kamal (sacred lotus), and Ambi (paisley/kalka) dividers.
   - **Cultural Terminology**: Solah Shringar, 100% Organic Sojat (Rajasthan) Henna, Dulha-Dulhan Storyboards, Pichwai Lotus, Laung ki Dhooni (clove smoke aftercare).

2. **Complete Sections & Pages**:
   - **Home**: Hero section, trust badges, key statistics (10+ years, 500+ brides, 100% organic), and dual CTAs.
   - **About Me**: Artist journey, Vedic roots, craft distinction, organic cone ritual, and 48-hour stain progression.
   - **Work / Portfolio**: Interactive category filters (*All, Bespoke Bridal, Indo-Arabic, Rajasthani, Feet & Anklets, Minimalist*), click-to-zoom Lightbox modal with complexity, duration, and direct WhatsApp design inquiry.
   - **Testimonials**: Verified royal destination bride reviews (Udaipur, Jaipur, London, Mumbai) with 5-star ratings.
   - **Packages & Aftercare**: Transparent service tiers (*The Imperial Maharani, Shahi Sangeet, Festive*) and traditional 4-step stain aftercare guide.
   - **Contact & WhatsApp Booking**: Comprehensive inquiry form converting client details into a formatted WhatsApp booking request.

3. **WhatsApp Integration**:
   - One-click direct booking via `https://wa.me/<number>?text=...`.
   - Floating 24/7 WhatsApp chat bubble.
   - **Instant Number Updater**: Click the *"Click here to set / update your WhatsApp number"* button on the contact section to update your number anytime without touching code!

4. **100% Responsive Design**:
   - Desktop & Laptop: Glassmorphic sticky header, multi-column layouts, smooth transitions.
   - Mobile: Slide-in navigation drawer, thumb-friendly sticky bottom action bar (*View Work* and *Book WhatsApp*).
