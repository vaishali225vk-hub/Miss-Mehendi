The Royal Aftercare Rituals (For Peak Dark Stain)

Authentic Sojat henna matures into rich mahogany through gentle heat and botanical care. Follow these 4 timeless Vedic steps:

01
Leave for 6–8 Hours

Allow the paste to dry naturally and settle deep into the skin layers before gentle removal.

02
Lemon-Sugar Glaze

We apply our infused lemon & demerara cane sugar glaze to keep the paste supple and active.

03
Laung Ki Dhooni

Infuse hands gently with warm clove smoke to activate the natural lawsone dye molecules.

04
Water Avoidance

Avoid water for the first 24 hours. Coat with natural coconut or mustard oil before bathing.
